Charts — fsharp-wasm-graphics

A line of ten million samples, a stream, a density map of five million points and two hundred thousand candles, drawn by the scene graph onto one canvas. A frame costs the pixels of the chart and not the points of the data: a minimum and maximum pyramid finds what a pixel column covers, a summed-area table counts the points in a cell.
