Accounting grid — fsharp-wasm-accounting

A ledger of one million journal lines as a pivot table, written in F# and compiled to WebAssembly GC. Rows and columns fold to their totals, the table turns (accounts, cost centres, periods), and the lines behind a cell open as a tab. The whole interface is drawn by the module onto one canvas; JavaScript only forwards pointer, wheel, key and size events.

Click a caret to open or fold a row or a column; arrow keys move, Space folds the row, Shift+Space the column, Enter (or a second click) opens the lines behind the cell as a tab, the wheel scrolls (Shift sideways). The number of lines, the time the table took to turn and the cost of a frame are in the README.
